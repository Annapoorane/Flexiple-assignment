Prototype-only representations

Company search records are synthetic scenarios, not verified legal entities. Partner cards are provider categories, not real vendors or endorsements. City strengths, relative cost descriptions, risks and fit scores are editorial hypotheses introduced to demonstrate the workflow. They are not source-backed quantitative forecasts.

Production evidence pipeline

Planned: source ingestion → entity resolution → human verification queue → versioned records → last-seen timestamp → source link → claim-level confidence → correction process. Distribution or storage of third-party datasets requires appropriate rights and licensing.
